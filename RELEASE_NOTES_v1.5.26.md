# Habit Builder Tracker v1.5.26 — Explicit Custom Focus Timer

- The Focus duration slider now has an explicit two-state rule: 0 uses the saved Focus preset; 1–59 minutes activates a custom Focus session.
- Returning the slider to 0 restores the saved Focus preset.
- The Focus timer label and slider status clearly indicate whether the preset or custom duration is active.
- Short and Long Break durations remain controlled by their saved preset settings.
- Custom selection persists locally and is locked while a session is active.
- Service-worker cache and package version updated to v1.5.26.
